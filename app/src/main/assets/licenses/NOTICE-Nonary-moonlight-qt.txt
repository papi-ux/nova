Nova's Android source-time mapper,

Drawn on: the wrap-aware RTP cadence step of VrrTimingController::observeCadence(), the bounded,
time-credited lower-envelope offset of VrrTimingController::observePlayoutOffset(), and the tick
conversion of VrrTimingController::rtpTicksToUs().

Nova's version is a much smaller state machine: only source mapping and a bounded playout reserve
(no presenter, latch or cadence smoothing), anchored on the streaming core's enqueue time on
Android, with the reset rules, reasons and replay fixtures of the shared Nova source-time
contract.

The design of the source-time request, and of carrying host frame timing to the client, is also
credited to Nonary's moonlight-qt and Vibeshine work:
